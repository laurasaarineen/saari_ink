Booking terms

Deposit

A non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment. The deposit will be deducted from the final tattoo price.

Rescheduling

Appointments may be rescheduled once with at least 48 hours notice. After the first reschedule, a new booking fee will be required. This fee will not be deducted from the final tattoo price.

Cancellations

Appointments can be cancelled with at least 48 hours notice. Cancellations made later than 48 hours before the appointment will be charged 50% of the agreed tattoo price.

No-show

A no-show means not attending your appointment without notice. In this case, 100% of the agreed tattoo price will be charged.

Design changes

Design adjustments are always possible, however I do not copy other artists' work. Major changes requested shortly before the appointment may require rescheduling.

Touch-ups

One free touch-up is included within 6 months of your appointment, provided that aftercare instructions have been properly followed.

Please note

You cannot get tattooed if:

  • You are under the age requirement (18+).
  • You are pregnant
  • You are under the influence of alcohol or drugs at the time of your appointment
  • The tattoo area is sunburned, irritated, infected, or damaged
  • You have a skin condition in the area or any condition that may affect healing (e.g. active eczema, rash, or infection)
  • You are ill or currently on a course of antibiotics (please note that any other medication should be discussed with a doctor)
