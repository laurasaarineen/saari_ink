FAQ

How much does a tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on design complexity, placement and size. Contact me for a custom quote. Minimum price is 100€ and full day rate (6h) is currently 500€.

How long does a session take?

Sessions vary from 1 to 6 hours depending on the design and detail level.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes, the studio is private and you're welcome to bring support.

What payment methods do you accept?

Card, Apple/Google Pay. Cash as well, please have exact change.
