About me

Hi, I'm Laura — I tattoo under the name saari_ink, working out of Saari Ink Tattoo Studio, my private studio in Tampere. I started tattooing in 2023, and my work spans custom blackwork, black & grey, and fine line, always built around pieces that feel personal, timeless, and unique to each client.

Comfort and a relaxed atmosphere are always the priority. Every tattoo is designed individually, everything is created just for you.

If you have an idea, I'd love to turn it into something real.
