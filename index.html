About me

Hi, I'm Laura. I started tattooing in 2023, and focus on custom, detailed designs with a strong black & grey aesthetic. My work is built around creating pieces that feel personal, timeless, and unique to each client.

I work from a private studio where comfort and a relaxed atmosphere are a priority. Every tattoo is designed individually, everything is created just for you.

If you have an idea, I'd love to turn it into something real.
