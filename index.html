About me

Hi, I'm Laura. I tattoo at my private studio in Tampere. I started tattooing in 2023, and my style is blackwork, black & grey, and fine line. My pieces are always personal, timeless, and unique to each client.

Creating a comfortable and relaxed atmosphere is always my priority. Every tattoo is designed individually and created just for you. This is a safe, inclusive, and judgment-free space where everyone is welcome.

If you have an idea, I'd love to turn it into something real.

The easiest way to book is through the booking form, but you're also welcome to get in touch via Instagram or by email at booking@saariink.com if you have any questions. Available booking dates are regularly shared on Instagram, but you can always ask about current availability directly.
